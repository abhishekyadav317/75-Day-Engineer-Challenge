# Expense Tracker CL

## Problem

Students spent money on many things like shopping , subscription , travel , food , education , groceries , etc . Expenses are dificult to remember , user rely on memory , write expenses on paper , check transaction history , donot show exact amount they spent . At the month end they may not know where their money went . this project provide into spending   



## Target User

My primary users are college students who struggle to track their daily expenses and want to understand where their monthly budget is being spent.

## Features

1.[] Add expense 
2.[] View expense 
3.[] Save data
4.[] calculate total spending
